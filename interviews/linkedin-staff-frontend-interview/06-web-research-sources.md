## Known Gaps (Confirm With Recruiter)

| Gap | Why it matters |
|-----|----------------|
| Staff **FE** vs Staff **SWE** phone screen format | Blind reports LC-heavy; FE write-ups report JS/UI-heavy |
| India vs US loop differences | Round count and DSA weight may differ |
| CoderPad vs plain text | Affects how you debug |
| React allowed or not | FE write-ups say vanilla JS only for early rounds |
| Exact elimination policy post-2025 | Some loops non-eliminatory after R1 — verify |

3. How many total rounds for this req?
