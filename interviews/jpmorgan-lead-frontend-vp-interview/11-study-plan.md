## Final Super Day Checklist

### Day Before
- [ ] Test camera, microphone, lighting
- [ ] Stable internet connection confirmed
- [ ] Quiet room with no interruptions booked
- [ ] Have a glass of water nearby
- [ ] Pen and blank paper ready for system design diagrams
- [ ] Sleep 7–8 hours

### Day Of
- [ ] Log in 10 minutes early
- [ ] Have your STAR story cheat sheet printed (for HireVue — you can reference notes)
- [ ] Between rounds: take 2 deep breaths; review one STAR story
- [ ] At the end of each round: thank the interviewer, ask your prepared question

### Mindset
- The coding round is not just "solve it" — it's "show your thinking process"
- The system design round wants trade-off discussion, not a perfect architecture
- The behavioral round is not an afterthought — it eliminates VP candidates who can't lead
- You're also interviewing them — use your prepared questions
