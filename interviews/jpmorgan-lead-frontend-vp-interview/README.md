## JP Morgan's 4 Business Principles (Memorize These)

Every behavioral question is evaluated through this lens:
1. **Exceptional client service** — delivering value to end users and stakeholders
2. **Operational excellence** — reliability, process improvement, efficiency
3. **Integrity, fairness, and responsibility** — doing the right thing, compliance, ethics
4. **Winning team culture** — collaboration, mentorship, inclusion, shared success

## Quick Tips

- **Security is non-negotiable** — this is finance. Know XSS, CSRF, CSP, CORS, JWT, RBAC deeply
- **Frame everything in financial context** — reliability, compliance, auditability, latency
- **All 3 Super Day rounds are elimination** — don't treat behavioral as a warmup
- **Know their open-source work** — mention Salt DS, Perspective, Modular to show genuine interest
- **"Why JP Morgan?"** — prepare a 2-minute answer mentioning fintech scale, open-source, and specific LOB
- **STAR method always** — for VP level, stories must show strategic decision-making, not just individual heroics
